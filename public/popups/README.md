# Popup artwork

Created with the built-in Imagegen tool on 2026-10-26 at the user's request.

- `welcome.png`: Thai marketplace welcome artwork.
- `support-website.png`: voluntary website support artwork with the account details supplied by the user.
- `shop-payment-qr.png`: unchanged copy of the QR supplied by the user; use this original for opening or saving the payment QR.

The support notice is voluntary website support, not payment for merchandise. Always check the recipient name in the banking app before confirming a transfer. These images and the QR must not be used for impersonation or fraud.

Prompts and verification are recorded in `docs/POPUP-ARTWORK.md`.
